Who we are

SunFlow ERP is provided by [Your company name], [address], contact: [e-mail / phone].

What the app stores

Business records you enter (customers, invoices, quotations, purchase bills, expenses, bank details printed on your documents, attachments, settings) are stored on your phone. We do not receive them.

What is sent to our server

  • Licence checks: licence ID, a random device ID, and the time of the check, so that subscriptions can be activated, renewed or suspended.
  • Password reset (optional): your mobile number and the e-mail address registered with your licence; a one-time code is e-mailed to you.
  • Cloud backup (optional, if you switch it on): an encrypted copy of your data. It is encrypted on your phone with a passphrase that only you know; we cannot read it.
  • Feedback (optional): the rating and text you submit.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data. The app requests only the INTERNET permission.

Your choices and deletion

You can export or erase your data at any time in the app (Settings › Backup, restore & export › Erase this company's data). To delete your licence record or e-mail address held on our server, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

Security

Licences are digitally signed; cloud backups are encrypted; the server keeps its administrator secret outside the app.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
